01

Information We Collect

We collect only what is needed to process your purchase and deliver your product. This includes:

  • Email address — provided when you complete a purchase. Used to deliver your access link and send order confirmations.
  • Name — provided during checkout. Used to personalise your access email.
  • Order information — order ID, product purchased, and purchase date. Stored to manage access and prevent duplicate deliveries.

We do not collect payment details. All payment processing is handled by Lemon Squeezy, our payment provider.

02

How We Use It

We use the information we collect to:

  • Deliver your purchased product via email.
  • Provide access link recovery if you lose your original email.
  • Send occasional product updates or announcements. You can opt out at any time by replying to any email.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

03

Data Storage

Purchase records (email, order ID, access token) are stored in Supabase, a hosted database platform. Data is held securely and is not shared beyond what is required to operate this service.

We retain your data for as long as your access to a product remains valid. If you wish to have your data deleted, contact us and we will remove your record.

04

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except as required to operate our services:

  • Lemon Squeezy — processes payments and shares order details with us to fulfil your purchase.
  • Resend — our email delivery provider. Your email address is used solely to send your access link and any order communications.
  • Supabase — stores your access token and order record.

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

05

Cookies

This website does not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar third-party tracking tools.

Basic browser storage (localStorage) may be used on product pages to remember your access token so you do not need to re-enter it on return visits. No personal data is transmitted to external services through this mechanism.

06

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt out of any email communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at info@appliedintelligencestudio.courses.

07

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our site or products after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

08

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, get in touch:

info@appliedintelligencestudio.courses

We aim to respond to all enquiries within 2 business days.
